How we build

Three principles we don't compromise on

They guide every feature we ship — and they're the criteria we use to retire features that no longer earn their place.

Accuracy first

Every model we ship — OCR, extraction, translation, summarisation — is tested against real-world scans before release. Not benchmarks designed to flatter the engine.

Structure matters

A document isn't useful as a blob of text. Tables need columns, invoices need named fields, reports need page context. We preserve structure at every step — OCR, conversion and export.

Transparent pricing

Free tools stay free. Pro features are priced by usage, not by seat. Enterprise contracts include self-hosted deployment — no surprise telemetry clauses.

Document intelligence

OCR that reads the structure, not just the pixels.

Most engines return a wall of characters. Ours returns fields, tables and relationships — invoice numbers, vendor names, dates, totals and line items — from the crooked, low-contrast scans that real work actually produces.
